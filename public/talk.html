How to test it
  1. Allow the microphone when asked, and use headphones if you can.
  2. Muster speaks first. Answer as the foreman would, in English or Spanish.
  3. Try the awkward answers too: a smaller crew, a late start, a missing pump truck.
  4. When it ends, open the call log to see what was recorded.
