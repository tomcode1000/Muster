What is Muster and how does it work?

Muster is a voice agent for construction projects. Every evening it calls each foreman booked for tomorrow, confirms crew size, arrival, prerequisites and deliveries, and turns the answers into a ranked list for the morning.

Is Muster free to use?

Yes. The Free plan includes up to 5 subcontractors and 30 call minutes a month, which is about 15 check-ins. Pro and Business raise both limits.

Can I connect my Google Sheets?

Yes. Share your sheet with Muster as an editor and paste its link. Muster reads the Crew and Tomorrow tabs before each round and writes each result back into the same row.

How secure is my data?

Your crew list, bookings and call logs are used only to run your check-ins. Every call is transcribed, so you can always see exactly what was said and what was recorded.

Can I use Muster for multiple teams?

Today a workspace runs one project, with its own crew, bookings and call schedule. Running several projects from one account is on the way.

What happens if I need help?

Send us a message through Contact Support and a person on our team will get back to you as soon as possible.

Do you offer a mobile app?

Muster runs in your phone's browser, so the plan, call logs and results work on site without installing anything. Foremen never need an app: they simply answer the call.
